# Eduma slice 10c — student services and grievances, placements and alumni, research and quality (BUILD-10, part 3)

**Date:** 25 October 2026 · **Status:** from the spec pack, fast mode. **Source:** 03 WF-OPS §11 (placement, alumni,
research, accreditation), 04 SCR-048, SCR-049, 08 ServiceCase / placement / research / accreditation aggregates,
09 `/service-cases`, `/placement/*`, `/research/*`, `/accreditation/*`; TST-034 and the BUILD-10 checklist rows.

## Decisions

| ID | Decision |
|---|---|
| S10C-01 | **Service cases** (one aggregate for requests and grievances, as the pack says): any member — staff, student, guardian — opens a case of an allowlisted type: service request, academic grievance, counselling, safeguarding. All but service requests are **confidential**. The server sets the requester, number (SC-0001), classification and response due date (7 / 15 / 3 / 1 days). The requester can't set an assignee, classification, internal notes or state. |
| S10C-02 | **Who sees what.** The requester sees their own cases: subject, description, a safe state, requester-visible notes and resolutions. The current assignee sees everything, including internal notes and the due date. Triage (`service_case.assign`) lists every case and assigns it; for a confidential case triage sees only the shell (number, type, state, dates, assignee), never the subject, description or notes, and search covers only ordinary cases. Anyone else gets 404. Reassignment removes the old assignee's access at once. A confidential case can only be assigned to someone holding `service_case.confidential`; assignment is explicit and audited, so an admin role alone discloses nothing. |
| S10C-03 | **Notes** are `requester_visible` or `internal`, checked on the server; requesters only ever write visible ones. Notifications say "update on SC-0001" with no excerpt. There is no case export. |
| S10C-04 | **States:** submitted → assigned → resolved → appealed → assigned → resolved. The assignee resolves with a requester-visible resolution. The requester may appeal once, within 15 days of the resolution; the appeal goes back to triage and must be decided by someone other than the original resolver. Every resolution is kept (original and appeal). Cases are not a messaging channel: notes are attached to the case only. |
| S10C-05 | **Placements** (`placement.manage`): employers, opportunities (job or internship; eligible programs, minimum CGPA, closing date). A student applies from the portal with explicit consent to share their profile with that employer; eligibility (program, CGPA from the progress calculation, deadline) is checked and snapshotted. applied → shortlisted → offered → accepted / declined (the student decides); rejected or withdrawn at any point before that. One application per opportunity per student. Reports count offers and accepted placements separately. No employer logins, and no student directory. |
| S10C-06 | **Internships:** accepting an internship offer creates the internship. The placement office assigns a staff supervisor, who alone (with `placement.manage`) records the evaluation. |
| S10C-07 | **Alumni:** a student creates their own alumni profile only by consenting (contact and occupation). Withdrawing the consent hides it at once. The placement office lists only consented profiles. |
| S10C-08 | **Research** (`research.manage`): scholars (a student with a topic), supervisors (main / co; ending a supervision ends access), ethics approvals with validity dates, and milestones. The scholar submits a milestone; one that needs ethics approval is refused (409 `ethics_required`) unless an approval is valid that day. A current supervisor approves or returns it, never the scholar. Visibility: the scholar sees their own record, supervisors see their scholars, research.manage sees everyone; anyone else gets 404. Grants and disbursements are not built (accounting adapter, D-11). |
| S10C-09 | **Quality evidence** (`quality_evidence.manage`): review cycles (framework, period). Evidence requests go to an owner per criterion. The owner uploads a file (PDF/PNG/JPEG checked by content, 5 MB; a failed upload records nothing), and each upload is a kept version with its SHA-256. The manager accepts or returns it, not the owner. A cycle freezes only when every request is accepted; it then stores a snapshot (criteria, version hashes, snapshot hash) and exports it as JSON. Nothing changes after freezing. The export is not an accreditation claim. |
| S10C-10 | Apps `cases` (module `services`) and `development` (modules `placements` for placements, internships and alumni; `quality` for research and evidence); the modules are available. Permissions: `service_case.assign`, `service_case.confidential` (powerful), `placement.manage`, `research.manage`, `quality_evidence.manage`. Roles: Student Services Officer (assign), Grievance Officer (assign + confidential), Placement Officer, Research Coordinator, IQAC Coordinator. |

## Tests

- **TST-034:** requester, assignee, triage and an unrelated staff member each list, search and read; internal notes never reach the requester; reassignment removes access; an appeal keeps the first resolution, a different reviewer decides it, and no second appeal is allowed.
- **Placements:** consent and eligibility are required; one application per opportunity; offers and accepted placements are reported apart; internship evaluation by the supervisor only; withdrawn alumni consent hides the profile.
- **Research:** ethics validity; supervisor-only approval; access ends with the supervision.
- **Quality:** an upload checked by content; acceptance by someone other than the owner; freezing refused while anything is missing; the frozen snapshot is reproducible.
- **Everything:** module gates and isolation.
